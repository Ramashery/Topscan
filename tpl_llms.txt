# TOPSCAN

> TOPSCAN — aerial geodesy (drone surveying) company based in Tbilisi, Georgia. RTK-corrected DJI Enterprise drones deliver survey data with 1–2 cm accuracy for construction, municipal/GIS, cadastral, agriculture and earthworks projects.

TOPSCAN produces point clouds (LAS / E57), orthophotos and elevation models (GeoTIFF), CAD drawings (DXF / DWG), GIS layers (GeoJSON) and volume reports (PDF / XLSX). Services include photogrammetry, LiDAR, orthophoto mapping, multispectral surveys, volume calculation and cadastral surveys. A free test flight (up to 0.5 ha) is offered before the main contract.
